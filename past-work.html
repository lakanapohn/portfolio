01 · GIS & watershed planning

Blue-green infrastructure
at the watershed scale.

The Blue Green Infrastructure Masterplan for Stormwater Management in the San Juan Bay Estuary Watershed explores nature-based solutions for ecological resilience and flood management. I used GIS to identify suitable drainage areas and support the restoration of ecological connections within the city.

02 · Stormwater systems & planting research

From urban drainage
to living infrastructure.

I researched tropical plants suitable for urban landscapes and their roles within blue-green infrastructure. The work connects planting choices with systems such as swales, bioretention basins, constructed wetlands, and detention ponds.

03 · 3D modelling & graphic design

Making water-sensitive
public spaces visible.

I created 3D models and diagrams to communicate how public spaces can manage water while supporting ecological benefits and environmental education. Exploded views, sections, and landscape models help explain the relationship between materials, planting, water, and people.
