About Me

I’m from Thailand and an MSc Applied Geoinformatics student at the University of Salzburg, with a background in Landscape Architecture and GIS-based environmental analysis.

I’m interested in using GIS and Earth Observation to understand environmental change and support sustainable decision-making. I enjoy interdisciplinary projects that connect geospatial technology, landscapes, and real-world environmental challenges.

My work focuses on GIS, cartographic design, remote sensing, and environmental analysis.

My interests include flood resilience, nature-based solutions, and Earth Observation for geospatial sustainability compliance.
