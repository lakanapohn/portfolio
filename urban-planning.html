Landscape Architecture · Urban Planning

Bang Lumphu
Chao Phraya Riverfront

Group urban planning studio project

Connecting the riverfront
with everyday public life.

This group project explores a connected public landscape along the Chao Phraya River in Bang Lumphu. Riverfront analysis leads to three proposals: a transition hub at Rama VIII Bridge, Sam Phraya Public Park, and the Military News School Museum Park. Each area brings a different mix of movement, recreation, and gathering to the riverside.

Location
Bang Lumphu, Bangkok, Thailand
Project
Group project · Studio 5
Focus
Urban connections, riverfront access, and public spaces

The riverfront

One riverfront,
three connected places.

The overall proposal considers the relationship between the river, neighbourhood streets, transport connections, and public spaces. A continuous riverside landscape connects the three project areas while allowing each place to respond to its own setting and activities.

The wider analysis brings together land use, movement, and public-space opportunities. The masterplan then locates the proposed interventions along the river.

The project areas

Three ways to meet the river.

Explore the separate plans, then open the analysis and detail boards for each area. The boards include the original zoning, sections, furniture, materials, and design perspectives.

Rama VIII Bridge transition hub

A linear riverside space connects movement with places to wait, rest, and meet. The proposal includes pier access, pavilions, kiosks, bicycle parking, and seating.

Sam Phraya Public Park

A neighbourhood park brings together play, outdoor fitness, courts, lawns, and resting areas beside the river.

Military News School Museum Park

The proposal connects museum and exhibition activities with an outdoor gallery, shaded gathering spaces, an amphitheatre, and recreation.
